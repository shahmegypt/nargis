## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, community-centric editorial platform tailored to the unique architectural and cultural fabric of New Cairo's Nargis 1 and Nargis 2 districts. The design narrative blends the tactile warmth of high-end independent publishing with the responsive precision of modern digital commerce.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
The interface evokes the sensory experience of a sun-drenched Saturday morning in an upscale Cairo suburb: fresh, warm, welcoming, and rooted in neighborhood pride. It avoids generic corporate marketplace tropes in favor of an artisanal magazine layout. It feels neighborly yet elevated, balancing everyday utility (finding dry cleaners, home bakers, clinics, and nursery ratings) with high-value local business advertising.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Tactile Paper Editorial:** Cream and warm stone backgrounds paired with sharp, deep-plum ink typography replicate print publications on uncoated matte paper stock.
- **Warm Minimalist Structure:** Clean geometric divisions and light hairline rules replace heavy drop shadows. Generous whitespace honors both Latin and Arabic scripts equally without visual clutter.
- **Vibrant Subtropical Accents:** Solar citrus, terracotta, and crisp mint sage provide purposeful focal points for calls to action, badges, and status markers without overwhelming the calm paper foundation.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from the natural terracotta brick, warm sandstone, and Mediterranean sun of Cairo, anchored by intense ink pigments that provide readability in harsh ambient daylight.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E75A45` - Terracotta Coral):** The primary signal color used for high-intent conversions, featured merchant outlines, active filter tabs, and promotional emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#FF9F1C` - Cairo Sun Amber):** Radiant secondary accent for rating stars, spotlight tiers, event indicators, and celebratory neighborhood milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#2EC4B6` - Fresh Mint Sage):** Crisp botanical contrast used for verified merchant credentials, open-now indicators, delivery availability, and positive reviews.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A1423` - Deep Midnight Ink):** The fundamental ink color for headings, primary text, and high-emphasis boundaries. Provides richer depth and warmth than raw `#000000`.
- **Neutral Supporting (`#2E1F38` - Plum Charcoal):** Secondary typography, metadata, unselected icons, and contextual subtitles.
- **Paper Canvas (`#FBF8F3` - Primary Base & `#F4EFE6` - Surface Tier):** Warm matte paper tones that replace clinical white to reduce eye strain and deliver an editorial publication backdrop.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes bilateral bilingual harmony between Modern Arabic and Latin scripts.

### Typographic Pairing
- **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Expressive, structural, and weighted. Its open apertures and crisp geometry seamlessly pair with high-character geometric Arabic typefaces (such as Alexandria and Cairo) during localized bidirectional rendering.
- **Body & Longform Reading (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, humanist, and highly readable at small-to-medium sizes, matching the x-height and fluid rhythm of contemporary Naskh/Kufic hybrid Arabic glyphs.
- **Labels, Metrics & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical precision for phone numbers, compound zone tags (e.g., `NARGIS-1 / ZONE-C`), price points, and review scores.

### Bidirectional & Arabic Rules
- In RTL (Arabic) contexts, all line heights expand by +10% over Latin baselines to provide sufficient vertical clearance for Arabic diacritics and ascenders/descenders.
- Letter spacing (`letter-spacing`) is strictly reset to `0` when rendering Arabic text to preserve script ligature integrity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial column system that transitions between structured directories and fluid lifestyle features.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (12 Columns | Breakpoint: 1200px+):** Outer margin of `margin-lg` (3.5rem) and gutter of `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content max-width is constrained to `1320px` to maintain comfortable eye scanning.
- **Tablet (8 Columns | Breakpoint: 768px - 1199px):** Outer margin of `margin-md` (2rem) and gutter of `gutter` (1.25rem). Multi-column business profiles fold into 2-column stacked listings.
- **Mobile (4 Columns | Breakpoint: < 768px):** Outer margin of `margin` (1rem) and gutter of `space-md` (1rem). Grid items convert into continuous vertical editorial cards with horizontal snapping carousels for spotlight directories.

### Spatial Rhythm
Padding relies strictly on the `space-*` scale. Component padding scales proportionally with content weight: badges and micro-tags utilize `space-xs` and `space-sm`, interactive inputs and buttons use `space-sm` vertical by `space-md` horizontal, and container cards employ `space-lg` to `space-xl` internally to preserve an uncluttered, premium editorial balance.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews synthetic drop shadows and glossy skeuomorphism in favor of structural tonal layering and warm tactile ambient depth.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Base (`#FBF8F3`):** The primary paper substrate upon which all main views and longform articles rest.
2. **Elevated Surface (`#F4EFE6`):** Used for standard listing cards, navigation bars, and search surfaces. Depth is established through subtle color variation rather than vertical blur.
3. **Floating & Interactive Overlays (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority popovers, search dropdowns, floating filter bars, and modal sheets sit on pure paper white, accompanied by an ultra-soft tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(26, 20, 35, 0.06)`. The midnight plum tint in the shadow preserves organic warmth.
4. **Editorial Framing:** Thin structural hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(26, 20, 35, 0.08)`) frame listing cards and category dividers, delivering the feel of a curated broadsheet publication.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve profile that softens structural interfaces without appearing juvenile.

### Application of Curves
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form fields, interactive buttons, listing thumbnail containers, and table layouts use consistent 8px corners.
- **Large Containers & Editorial Cards (`1rem` / 16px):** Merchant display banners, sponsored story hero blocks, and filter panels.
- **Pills & Status Badges (`9999px`):** Metric badges, zone selectors (Nargis 1 vs Nargis 2), sponsored labels, and interactive chips use full organic pill silhouettes to stand out sharply from rectangular content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call to Order / Inquire / Book):** Solid `#E75A45` (Terracotta Coral) fill, bold `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to deeper coral (`#D44A36`) with micro-elevation.
- **Secondary Action (Directions / WhatsApp / Share):** Outlined with `1.5px solid #1A1423`, interior transparent, text `#1A1423`. Hover shifts fill to `rgba(26, 20, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Ghost style with no border, midnight plum text, and a soft tint background on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **District Switcher:** Dual segmented pill toggle (`Nargis 1 | النرجس ١` vs `Nargis 2 | النرجس ٢`). Unselected uses transparent background with `#2E1F38` text; selected transitions to `#1A1423` with `#FBF8F3` text.
- **Sponsored Advertisement Badge:** High-end micro-capsule. Background `#FFF4E5`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 159, 28, 0.4)`, text `#C87400` in `label-sm` Space Grotesk.
- **Review & Trust Badges:** Pill-shaped, background `#E8F8F5`, text `#1A7A70` paired with `#2EC4B6` star icons.

### Directory & Advertising Cards
- **Standard Business Card:** Background `#F4EFE6`, border `1px solid rgba(26, 20, 35, 0.07)`, `1rem` radius. Includes 16:9 featured imagery, title in `headline-sm`, category tag, open status indicator (`#2EC4B6` dot), and instant contact trigger.
- **Sponsored Spotlight Card:** Background `#FFFFFF` framed by a subtle dual border (`2px solid #FF9F1C` header rule, hairline `#E5E0D8` perimeter). Includes elevated editorial pull-quotes and business highlights.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Tall input height (48px-52px), background `#F4EFE6`, border `1px solid rgba(26, 20, 35, 0.12)`, text `#1A1423` in `body-md`. Focus state shifts background to `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `2px solid #E75A45` ring.
- **RTL Alignment:** Input text automatically right-aligns for Arabic entry, swapping clear and search icon positions without layout collapse.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom 20px squares and circles with a `2px solid #2E1F38` outline. Active state fills with `#E75A45` and displays a crisp white check or center pip.
